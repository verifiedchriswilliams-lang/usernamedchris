About

I've spent twenty-plus years building things people use every day: streaming platforms, subscription businesses, podcast networks, consumer apps. Along the way I've been a COO, a President, a General Manager, and Chief Product Officer, which is a long way of saying I've run product, technology, growth, and operations, usually all at the same time.

iHeartRadio · GM & Chief Product Officer

I transitioned from a legacy media executive to a fully fledged product and technology leader, ultimately serving as the GM & Chief Product Officer for over 7 years during their largest streaming and podcast growth periods.

The Daily Wire · COO · Chief Digital Officer

Most recently I was COO of The Daily Wire, where my job was to make strategy, execution, and the numbers agree with each other.

Here's what I believe

Growth is the byproduct of a product with a clear vision that keeps its promises.

Figure out the role your audience needs you to play in their lives, then build the whole company around delivering on it, over and over. Every kept promise deepens trust and strengthens the consumer relationship. Every broken promise takes you back to square one.

That's how I think about product, brand, leadership, and operations. Not distinct silos, but one system that aligns to build something people love and feel is essential in their lives.
